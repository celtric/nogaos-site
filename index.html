NoGa OS

NoGa OS is a virtual operating system for kids: a complete desktop with its own windows, files and apps. It helps kids learn real computer basics in a controlled space: mouse, keyboard, windows, files, and simple educational/play apps. It is intentionally not a real operating system: the host does the low-level work, so all the effort goes into what a kid actually sees and touches. And it is a gateway rather than a destination: a kid outgrows it in a few years and moves on to Windows, macOS or Linux, so NoGa OS keeps to the habits those desktops share, made bigger, fewer and plainer, instead of inventing its own.

Running it

Install Java 17 or newer, then run the downloaded file:

java -jar nogaos-198.jar

The first start asks you to create an account. Video and audio playback need VLC installed on the computer; nothing else does.

A computer of their own

An old laptop or a Raspberry Pi can become a NoGa OS computer: it powers on straight into NoGa OS, with no login prompt and no Linux desktop reachable behind it. Install Debian (the small "netinst" image, with no desktop environment selected) or Raspberry Pi OS Lite, log in, and run:

wget -qO install-kiosk.sh https://celtric.github.io/nogaos-site/install-kiosk.sh && sudo bash install-kiosk.sh

It installs Java and everything else NoGa OS needs, downloads the latest version and sets the computer to start it on boot. It is a plain script you can read first, and running it again is safe.

Status

This is a personal project, written for my own children and shared in case it is useful to anyone else. It is early and it has rough edges. If you try it, bug reports and comments are welcome.

Changelog

Version 198 · 10 October 2026

  • **Media Player** and the **Voice Recorder** (the Sound Recorder's new name) wear the same plain look: big square buttons, a blue progress bar you click to jump, no volume slider or menus a kid never used, a framed picture for a video and a small fixed window for a sound; two players open at once no longer swap their files, and clicking the bar of a stopped video shows that moment, ready to play
  • Recording your voice: a live level bar moves with it in the **Voice Recorder** and the **Worksheet Editor**, and the click of the **Stop** button is cut off the end of every recording; the Voice Recorder asks before a recording that was not saved is lost
  • The Control Panel's pictograms applet is now called **ARASAAC** and wears their logo
  • Lists with column headers (the **Zip Viewer**, the activity log, the licences, the print queue) are framed as one piece, header included, with the scroll bar beside them

Version 197 · 9 October 2026

  • **Zip files**: double-click one to see what is inside and open its files, **Extract to...** unpacks it where you choose, and **Send to › Zip file** bundles the selected files; **Send to** can also put a shortcut on the desktop
  • Select several files at once with `Ctrl` on the desktop and in the explorer, and everything with `Ctrl+A`
  • **Worksheets**: a worksheet can carry its own picture as the file's icon, new pages with a video or a triangle to play with, the teacher decides what each page says aloud, and the new-page dialog has an **All** tab with search and page previews
  • The Control Panel's **Microphone** tab no longer cuts its texts, shows a coloured level bar, and records a short test you can play back
  • The **PDF Viewer**'s pages pane looks like the Worksheet Editor's, and the **Save** button in **WordPad** and the **Worksheet Editor** is greyed out until something changed

Version 196 · 8 October 2026

  • Shortcuts keep working when the file or folder they open is moved or renamed, anywhere on your own drive or the Shared drive
  • New **Microphone** tab in the Control Panel's **Sounds**, to set the recording level while watching a live meter; a microphone left at full volume is turned down once, so recordings no longer come out loud and crackly
  • **Worksheets** can be written in Italian, and the player turns to the next page by itself once a page is solved

Version 195 · 8 October 2026

  • New **Worksheets** and **Worksheet Editor** apps, which replace **Parla**: teachers, parents and older kids make worksheets page by page, choosing from sounds-and-words, information and maths pages and filling them with pictures, words and their own recorded voices, and a kid double-clicks the file to play it one page at a time
  • New **Pictograms** applet in the Control Panel: a grown-up downloads ARASAAC's pictograms once, the pictures and the words of each language on their own, and apps such as the Worksheet Editor use them without the internet
  • Each user's own **Start Menu** folder adds shortcuts, files and submenus to **Start → Programs**, and right-clicking anything in the Start menu offers **Open** and **Edit Start menu**

Version 194 · 6 October 2026

  • New **Parla** app by Marta Boluda: first vocabulary in Catalan for young children — eleven themes of words with pictograms and voice, model sentences, a story with questions and fifteen small activities, with each child's progress kept their own
  • **JClic** plays whole bundles: a project's sub-projects download with it and its menus lead into them and back; fill-in-the-blanks texts now play too
  • Voices answer at once: a word already said plays instantly, and while an app that speaks is open its voice stays ready instead of reloading on every click; Languages prepares the words of the open topic ahead of time
  • Changes in the Control Panel's **Internet** settings (home page, allowed or blocked sites) apply to the open browser the moment you press **OK**
  • The **Downloads** folder has its own icon and sits at the top of **Start → Documents**; the empty **Website shortcut** entry is gone from the **New** menu (make shortcuts from the browser instead); a link to Bret Victor's *Inventing on Principle* lives on the System drive

Version 193 · 6 October 2026

  • New **JClic** app: plays the activity packs Catalan teachers make — matching, memory, finding places on a picture, pages read aloud — browsing and downloading the zonaClic library of more than 2,600 projects by language, level and subject, with each child's downloads and progress kept their own; not every kind of activity plays yet, and **Help → Compatibility** says which
  • **Print** a picture from the picture viewer, and **Print picture** from a web picture's right-click menu in the Internet Browser

Version 192 · 5 October 2026

  • **Shared files** in a NoGaOS Cloud network: a provider can offer a network one shared folder, kept by the provider, that every computer of the network reads and writes like a drive from the network folder in **My Computer**; deleting there is permanent for the kids, and the provider's web console lists deleted files to restore them, browses, downloads, renames and deletes
  • **One network folder** in My Computer: the computers on the Wi-Fi and the people of the cloud network are one list under the network's own name ("Escola", "Casa"; **Local Network** without a provider), each computer once, and a session goes over the Wi-Fi when it can and through the provider otherwise
  • New **Trigonometry** and **Space** apps: two books of six pages to play with — an angle to turn, a triangle, a slide, a Ferris wheel, its wave and a ladder; and day and night, the year and the seasons, the Moon, the Sun, the planets and the Milky Way — each page a picture with a sentence that follows what you do

Version 191 · 4 October 2026

  • **Mail through NoGaOS Cloud**: a provider's administrator gives each person of a network (the home, the school) an account and links it to their user on the computers they use; those users then write to the people of their network from the **Mail** app, find the same mail on every computer, and see who they are in the cloud from a new tray icon. The provider's web console grew pages for networks, people and mailboxes, a computer now belongs to one provider and one network, and what its computers are offered (backups, mail) is decided per network
  • **Mail** app: a plainer layout with Inbox and Sent tabs and the chosen letter read beside the list, and notes to yourself are allowed
  • Sessions between computers open as a small chat window that grows when an activity starts, with the activities in a menu; sending a file is now an activity the other kid accepts or declines after seeing the file's name and size

Version 190 · 2 October 2026
